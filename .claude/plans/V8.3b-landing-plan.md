# V8.3b: the landing as a website (plan; direction E mocked, build pending Hmz's go)

> **Round 3 (current): E, Jake Presents** (`mockups/e.html`, `shots/sheet-e.png`). It supersedes D (sections 2 to 4
> below) where they differ; sections 5 to 8 still apply.
> - **The page is A.** Jake presents the features: in each section he appears on his own (no room) beside that
>   section's short motion graphic, and does something with it.
> - **Each graphic is small.** It starts when its section enters view and plays at its own pace; nothing is pinned
>   or scrubbed.
> - **The room is one section, "Immersive" ("Step Into the Classroom"):** the culmination. The camera tours the room
>   (zooms in and out, looks around) while Jake teaches, with the diagram, the model and the desk question in place.
>   You can drag to look around.
> - **The sections:**
>   - Hero: waves hello.
>   - A Teacher of Your Own: holds the idea while the colonnade draws itself.
>   - It Finds the Ideas: thinks, then points as the ideas link.
>   - It Draws the Picture: points while the diagram resolves from noise.
>   - It Builds the Model: presents while the picture lifts into points onto the real heart, which you can turn.
>   - Five Moves: one gesture per move.
>   - It Remembers: taps each review point as the curve lifts; plain labels; the map below.
>   - Immersive.
>   - For Parents: the same look as everything else (Hmz).
>   - Close: waves goodbye each time it enters view.
> - **Technically:** one transparent canvas carries Jake between section spots. Each section's 3D piece renders
>   beside him. The room loads only near Immersive. On phones Jake is still images of his poses.
> - **No card is tilted.** The skew Hmz saw was the mockups' rotate(-2deg) on the quiz card; the product's desk card
>   has no roll.
>
> **Approved by Hmz (2026-09-30), with two hard requirements:**
> 1. **Nothing may look wrong.** The mockups' hero Jake looked oddly slim: that was the lab capture's framing, not
>    the design. In the build Jake is the product's own `Teacher`, rendered live. Before any section ships, compare

>    scale.

## 4. Jake and the 3D

- **One live stage, three windows:** the hero, the lesson and the close. As in V8.3, it is one fixed canvas clipped
  to whichever window is on screen. Its clip follows that window's box (positioning only; nothing about the story is
  tied to the scroll).
- **A camera pose per window:** hero idle, the lesson's move poses, close. When no window is on screen the stage
  stops rendering. A window that is not the live one shows its poster.
- **Why three:** the hero sells "your own teacher" at first sight (B). The lesson is the immersion, deliberate and
  emphasised: the only full-bleed moment on the page. The close is his goodbye.
- **Everywhere else the lesson is referenced, not replayed:** A's objects in section 3, the map in section 5.
- **Phones and weak GPUs (lite):** the windows are stills, and section 4 cross-fades its five move stills on the same
  clock. The stills are recaptured with the new face and without the placeholder paper.

## 5. Performance plan

- **First load at most 135 kB** (today 127). The scroll driver, the pinned frames, the scene-time timeline and
  `HeartBuild` go. The rest is IntersectionObserver hooks and small clock-driven players (CSS transitions and class
  swaps; no Motion or GSAP). Measured from `yarn build`.
- **LCP:** the H1, as text (Archivo through `next/font`, size-adjusted fallback). The hero poster is WebP with fixed
  dimensions and high priority, so if it measures as the LCP it is early too. Measured at every width and theme.
- **No layout shift:** every window and media box has fixed dimensions, motion is transform, opacity and clip-path
  only, and the nav is fixed height. Target CLS 0.
- **Heavy code after first paint:**
  - The stage chunk (about 300 kB of JS, then the room 1.05 MB, Jake 2.28 MB, his clips 0.74 MB, and the heart 624 kB
    when the lesson section nears) loads on the full path after `load` and an idle moment.
  - It is warmed hidden before showing (`warm.ts`). Sound loads only on "Hear it".
- **Modes kept:**
  - `full` on desktops.
  - `lite` on phones, weak GPUs and Save-Data: stills.
  - `stack` under reduced motion or no JS: nothing plays.
  - The stage hands over to lite if it is slow, throws, or its chunk fails.
- **Frame budget:**
  - The V8.3 lessons hold: lazy parts warmed before use, no per-frame SVG filters, pause state in a small host, idle
    writers skipped.
  - The caption touches one word per change.
  - Measured headless on the GPU: frame times while the lesson plays and during a steady scroll at 360 / 768 / 1024 /
    1280 / 1440 in both themes, plus lite under a 4x CPU throttle.
