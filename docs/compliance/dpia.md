## 1. Description

Aristo is an AI tutor for grades 6 to 8 (ages about 11 to 14). A 3D teacher avatar explains topics generated by language
models, shows pictures and models, and quizzes the learner. Mastery is tracked per concept with spaced repetition. A
rule-based profile (expertise, depth, pace, example style) is inferred from time, clicks and quiz accuracy. Operator: one
individual in Austria. Data and flows: `records-of-processing.md`. Providers: `processors.md`.

## 2. Necessity and proportionality

- Purpose is specific: teaching and scheduling reviews. Contract with the parent is the basis; consent for optional
  features (voice, custom teacher, free chat) given by the parent.
- Minimisation: first name or nickname, no camera or location, no names or emails sent to AI providers, no analytics, no
  advertising, no sale. To do: stop storing typed topics in the request log; shorten free-text retention.
- Accuracy: AI content can be wrong. Mitigation: label on every lesson, terms, report-a-problem link.
- Rights: self-service download and delete in `/account` (built in parallel), contact address, one-month answer.

## 3. Risks and measures

| Risk | Likelihood | Severity | Measures | Residual |
